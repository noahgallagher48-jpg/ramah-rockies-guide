The donor book42 frames

The selection built for the donor book.

Every frame carries the largest size it prints at on each material: metal and acrylic hold the most detail and ask the most resolution, photo paper a little less, canvas least because the weave and the viewing distance forgive it. These are stock sizes, orderable as listed with no custom cutting, and each one matches that frame's own proportions, so nothing is cropped to fit. A few frames print true only as a custom cut; those say so instead of naming a size.
